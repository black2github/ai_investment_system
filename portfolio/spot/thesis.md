# SPOT — thesis v1.0

**Status:** company model only; no valuation/MC calibration in this party. Trigger ≠ Decision.

Spotify is modeled as a scaled global internet audio platform with two monetization engines: Premium subscriptions and Ad-Supported. Q2 2026 shows a favorable combination of double-digit audience growth, Premium ARPU/pricing gains, gross-margin expansion and strong free cash flow, while advertising revenue materially lags ad-supported audience growth. The model therefore separates audience scale from Premium monetization and ad monetization rather than compressing all growth into one axis.

The key thesis tests are: (1) can MAU/subscriber growth remain healthy as the base approaches one billion users; (2) can Premium pricing/ARPU rise without subscriber-growth erosion; (3) can advertising revenue catch up with the ad-supported audience; (4) can gross-margin gains persist despite music/podcast/audiobook rights economics and product/cloud/AI reinvestment; and (5) can operating profit and FCF remain structurally positive.

`INTERNET_PLATFORMS` is proposed as a **provisional** sector assignment. No approved benchmark is asserted.

Primary sources: Spotify Q2 2026 Form 6-K / IAS 34 interim report and Q2 Update (2026-08-04), plus FY2025 Form 20-F.
